WHY MOST AD CAMPAIGNS WASTE MONEY

Most agencies just boost posts and hope for the best. We test multiple video ads and track every customer to make sure your ads make a profit.

The Typical "Boost Post" Mistake
  • Broken Tracking: No conversion tracking, so you have no idea which ads brought sales or leads.
  • Boring & Tired Ads: Running the same static image for months until people ignore it.
  • No Follow-Up Ads: Showing cold ads repeatedly with no special offer for interested buyers.
  • Wasting Ad Budget: Guessing budgets randomly without knowing your true profit margins.
The Socio Ninjas Approach
  • Accurate Tracking: Full setup so you know exactly which ads bring leads, calls, and purchases.
  • Fresh Creative Ads: Testing 15+ video and image ads every week to find the biggest winners.
  • Smart Follow-Up Ads: Reminding warm prospects and website visitors with special offers.
  • Scaled with Control: Gradually increasing budget only on winning ads that produce high returns.
