SMARTER INFLUENCER MARKETING

WHY MOST INFLUENCER DEALS WASTE MONEY

Paying influencers for a 24-hour story without a clear plan rarely brings sales. Here is how our approach delivers real business results.

Typical Agency Approach
  • Fake or Inactive Followers: Paying high fees to influencers whose followers are bots or unengaged.
  • 24-Hour Stories That Disappear: Spending your budget on temporary posts that vanish by tomorrow with zero lasting value.
  • No Rights to Use Videos: Not having legal permission to reuse creator videos as paid ads on Meta or Google.
  • Vague Instructions: Leaving creators on their own, leading to awkward promotions that nobody buys.
The Socio Ninjas Approach
  • Verified, Real Followers: We screen every creator to verify genuine engagement, real local audiences, and quality comments.
  • Full Video Rights: You own the rights to run winning creator videos as high-performing Instagram and Facebook ads.
  • Clear Script Guidelines: We give creators strong hooks, storytelling ideas, and clear calls to action that drive clicks.
  • Performance-Focused Deals: Fair contracts where creators are motivated to deliver real engagement and sales.
