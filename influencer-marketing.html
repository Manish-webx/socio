INFLUENCER FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers about creator vetting, whitelisting, product gifting, and campaign ROAS.

We run every prospective creator through Modash and proprietary data scrapers to inspect follower growth history, audience location, bot percentage, and comment authenticity. We only partner with creators whose authentic engagement score exceeds 85%.
Whitelisting (Partnership Ads) allows us to run paid Meta and TikTok ads directly through the influencer’s verified social handle rather than your brand page. This leverages the creator's organic trust and typically cuts customer acquisition costs (CAC) by 30% to 50%.
Socio Ninjas handles the entire end-to-end process: creator discovery, outreach, contract negotiation, digital signature execution, briefing, content QA, and performance tracking. You only need to supply product samples for shipping.
For maximum ROI and direct sales, micro-influencers (15k to 100k followers) and nano-influencers (5k to 15k) provide the highest engagement rates and lowest cost per acquisition. We combine them with paid whitelisting to achieve macro-level reach at a fraction of the cost.
Each creator is assigned a personalized discount coupon code and custom UTM tracking link. We integrate these directly into your Shopify/WooCommerce store and Triple Whale dashboard to track sales, average order value, and ROAS in real-time.
