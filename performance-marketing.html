GROWTH FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about full-funnel performance marketing and blended growth.

Ads management only buys traffic on a single ad platform. Performance marketing manages the entire revenue lifecycle: multi-channel acquisition across Meta and Google, custom advertorial landers (CRO), and email/SMS retention funnels (Klaviyo) to maximize overall profitability and customer lifetime value.
Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) is calculated as Total Net Revenue divided by Total Ad Spend across all channels. Because ad platforms like Meta and Google often take credit for the same conversion, platform ROAS is distorted. MER gives you the real, unfiltered truth about your bottom-line profitability.
For a healthy e-commerce or B2C brand, Klaviyo email and SMS should generate between 25% and 40% of total monthly revenue. If your retention flows generate under 20%, you are overpaying for customer acquisition and leaving pure profit on the table.
We recommend a minimum ad budget of ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000/month. This budget allows us to run prospecting on Meta, capture intent on Google Search, and feed sufficient conversion volume into your automated retention sequences.
We set up server-side Conversions API (CAPI) on Meta, Enhanced Conversions on Google, and first-party attribution tracking platforms like Triple Whale / Hyros to map each customer touchpoint from first click to repeat purchase.
